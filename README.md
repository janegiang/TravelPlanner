# Travel Planner

A web application that helps users plan, organize, and keep track of their future trips in one convenient place.

## Overview

Travel Planner allows users to build and manage their travel plans by saving destinations, creating personalize itineraries, and keeping important flight information organized.

The goal is to make trip planning simple by bringing destinations, schedules, and flight details together in one place.

## Features

### 📍Save Locations

- Search for and save locations that users want to visit.
- Keep a collection of potential destinations for future trips.
- Visualize saved locations with interactive maps.

### 🗓️ Create an Itineraty

- Create an itinerary for each trip.
- Add saved locations and activities to an itinerary.
- Organize plans by date and time.

### ✈️ Flight Information

- Add flight information to a trip.
- Store departure and arrival details.
- Track scheduled departure and arrival times.
- View the current flight status.
- Keep flight information alongside the rest of the itinerary.

## How It Works

1. Create a trip

   Start by creating a new trip and providing basic information such as the destination and travel dates.

2. Add flight information

   Enter your flight details to keep departure time, arrival time, and flight status accessible from your trip.

3. Save locations

   Add destinations and places you'd like to visit during your trip.

4. Visualize your trip

   View your saved locations as pins on a map and organize them into your itinerary.

5. Build your itinerary

   Organize saved locations and activities into a day-by-day itinerary.

6. Manage your trip

   Return to your trip at any time to update locations, itinerary items, or flight information.
