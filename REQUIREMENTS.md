# Project Requirements: Trip Planner Website

## 1. Overview
A web application designed to help travelers plan their trips by creating personalized daily itineraries, tracking flight statuses, and saving travel plans to their accounts.

## 2. Requirements
* **Authentication:** Users must be able to sign up for an account and log in securely so that their data and saved itineraries persist.
* **Flight Tracking:** Users must be able to input a flight number and departure date to retrieve and track flight status information.
* **Destination Input:** Users must be able to search specific locations, attractions, or restaurants they want to visit.
* **Itinerary Management:** Users can organize and schedule their saved locations into structured, day-by-day travel itineraries.


## 3. User Stories
* **As a** new traveler, **I want to** sign up for an account and log in **so that** my saved itineraries and preferences are stored for future visits.
* **As an** air traveler, **I want to** input my flight number and departure date **so that** I can track my flight details within the app.
* **As a** traveler exploring a new city, **I want to** search and save locations I want to visit **so that** I can easily reference them later.
* **As a** trip organizer, **I want to** drag or assign my saved locations into daily schedules **so that** I have a clear, day-by-day itinerary for my trip.

## 4. Out of Scope (Version 1.0)
* Direct booking integrations for hotels, flights, or rental cars.
* Collaborative real-time editing of itineraries with multiple users.
* Budget calculation and expense management tools.
